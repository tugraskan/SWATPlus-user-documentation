# carbon

**Code reference:** `sd_chd(idb)%carbon` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_module.f90#L33)).

Carbon content of the channel bank and bed material, entered as a percent. SWAT+ combines carbon/100 with a pesticide's Koc value to calculate its channel sediment-water partition coefficient.
