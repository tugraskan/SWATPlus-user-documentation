# carbon

**Code reference:** `sd_chd(idb)%carbon`

**Declared type:** `swatdeg_hydsed_data` ([reader](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/sd_hydsed_read.f90#L61), [declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/sd_channel_module.f90#L33)).

Carbon content of the channel bank and bed material, entered as a percent. SWAT+ combines carbon/100 with a pesticide's Koc value to calculate its channel sediment-water partition coefficient.
