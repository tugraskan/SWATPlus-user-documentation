# dp

**Code reference:** `sd_chd(idb)%chd` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_module.f90#L22)).

The channel depth is defined as the depth of the channel from the top of the channel bank to the channel bottom. It is calculated by QSWAT+ using a regression equation relating the channel depth to the drainage area of the channel.
