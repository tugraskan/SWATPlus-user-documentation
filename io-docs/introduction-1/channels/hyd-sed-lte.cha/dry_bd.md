# dry\_bd

**Code reference:** `sd_chd(idb)%ch_bd` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_module.f90#L34)).

Dry bulk density of channel bank and bed material in t/m3. SWAT+ uses it in eroded-sediment mass calculations and in the channel-sediment porosity calculation.
