# slp

**Code reference:** `sd_chd(idb)%chs`

**Declared type:** `swatdeg_hydsed_data` ([reader](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/sd_hydsed_read.f90#L61), [declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/sd_channel_module.f90#L23)).

The channel slope is defined as the average slope of the channel along its length. It is calculated by QSWAT+ based on the DEM.
