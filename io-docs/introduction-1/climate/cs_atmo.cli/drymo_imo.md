# drymo(imo)

**Code reference:** `atmodep_cs(iadep)%cs(ics)%drymo(imo)` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/climate_module.f90#L227)).

Dry deposition over the month in kg/ha. The daily input is $M/D$, where $M$ is the supplied amount and $D$ is the number of days in the month. See [cs_rain.f90](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_rain.f90#L42-L82).
