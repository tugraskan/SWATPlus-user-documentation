# dryyr(iyr)

**Code reference:** `atmodep_cs(iadep)%cs(ics)%dryyr(iyr)` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/climate_module.f90#L229)).

Dry deposition over the year in kg/ha. SWAT+ divides by 365 to obtain the daily input. See [cs_rain.f90](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_rain.f90#L42-L82).
