# org\_p

**Code reference:** `fertdb(it)%forgp` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/fertilizer_data_module.f90#L10)).

Mass fraction of the fertilizer that is organic P, in kg P per kg fertilizer. The soil pools that receive it depend on the carbon setting and `rtof` ([`pl_fert.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/pl_fert.f90#L91-L136)).
