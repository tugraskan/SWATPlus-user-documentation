# aq\_hlife

**Code reference:** `pestdb(ip)%aq_hlife`

**Declared type:** `pesticide_db` ([reader](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/pest_parm_read.f90#L48), [declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/pesticide_data_module.f90#L12)).

Half-life of the pesticide in the water, in days. SWAT+ adjusts it for water temperature when it computes pesticide decay in channels and aquifers.
