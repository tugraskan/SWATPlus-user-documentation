# aq\_volat

**Code reference:** `pestdb(ip)%aq_volat` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/pesticide_data_module.f90#L13)).

Volatilized mass is the coefficient × dissolved fraction × pesticide mass ÷ water depth, in channels ([`ch_rtpest.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_rtpest.f90#L144-L150)) and reservoirs ([`res_pest.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_pest.f90#L90-L97)).
