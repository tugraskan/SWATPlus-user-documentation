# ben\_bury

**Code reference:** `pestdb(ip)%ben_bury` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/pesticide_data_module.f90#L18)).

Buried mass is `ben_bury × benthic mass / ben_act_dep`, removed from the active layer in channels ([`ch_rtpest.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_rtpest.f90#L193-L199)) and reservoirs ([`res_pest.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_pest.f90#L163-L170)).
