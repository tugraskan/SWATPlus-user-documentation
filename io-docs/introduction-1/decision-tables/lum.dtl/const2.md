# const2

**Code reference:** `dtbl_lum(i)%act(iac)%const2` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/conditional_module.f90#L24)).

Secondary numeric value. The reader raises any entry below 1 to 1. [`dtbl_lum_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/dtbl_lum_read.f90#L97)

For `irrigate`, `fertilize`, `manure`, `till`, `harvest`, `kill`, `harvest_kill`, `pest_apply`, `tiledep_control`, `puddle`, `burn`, `cn_update`, and `pheno_reset`, its integer part is the maximum number of times the row acts per calendar year; the counter resets at year end. `plant` and `graze` count but do not check the limit. `irr_demand` checks the limit but never advances the counter, so it is not limited. [`actions.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/actions.f90#L143-L167) · [`actions.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/actions.f90#L268-L268) · [`actions.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/actions.f90#L455-L514) · [`hru_control.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_control.f90#L203-L208)

Other uses: day of year for `fert_future`, channel order for `chan_change`, and, with the action name `ponding`, the ponded depth in mm below which irrigation is triggered (so values under 1 mm become 1).
