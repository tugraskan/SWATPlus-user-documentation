# chan_length

**Code reference:** `chan_length` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_chan_read.f90#L28), [read](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_chan_read.f90#L61)).

Length of the channel inside the cell, in m. The exchange area is the channel width times this length ([`gwflow_channel_exch.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_channel_exch.f90#L72)). The floodplain exchange of a cell that is also a channel cell is reduced by the same area ([`gwflow_floodplain.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_floodplain.f90#L66-L74)).
