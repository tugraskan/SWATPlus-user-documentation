# wet

**Code reference:** `gw_wet_flag` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_module.f90#L301), [read](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_read.f90#L248)).

1 turns the process on; 0 (or a missing setting) leaves it off. When on, groundwater flows into wetlands whose stage is below the water table, and wetland water seeps to the aquifer otherwise ([`gwflow_wetland.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_wetland.f90#L68-L72)). Every wetland starts with a bed thickness of 0.25 m; [gwflow.wetland](../gwflow.wetland/README.md) can replace it ([`gwflow_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_read.f90#L1165-L1171)).

SWAT+ sets it to 0 when LSU links are used ([`gwflow_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_read.f90#L341), [`gwflow_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_read.f90#L353)).
