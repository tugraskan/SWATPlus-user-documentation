# poly_area

**Code reference:** `poly_area` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_read.f90#L155), [read](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_read.f90#L2108)).

Area of the intersection between the LSU and the cell, in m². Divided by [lsu_area](lsu_area.md), it gives the cell's share of the LSU's recharge and ET ([`gwflow_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_read.f90#L2115), [`gwflow_gwet.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_gwet.f90#L46-L47)).
