# res_cell

**Code reference:** `res_cell` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_read.f90#L110), [read](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_read.f90#L1146)).

Number of a cell covered by the reservoir. Its ground elevation is used as the reservoir head, and its neighbors in [cellcon.gw](../cellcon.gw/README.md) are the cells that exchange water with the reservoir ([`gwflow_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_read.f90#L1148-L1155), [`gwflow_reservoir.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_reservoir.f90#L45-L51)). With `grid_type` = `structured`, SWAT+ maps cell numbers through an identity list, so structured and unstructured grids both use cell numbers 1 to `ncell` ([`gwflow_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_read.f90#L313-L319)).
