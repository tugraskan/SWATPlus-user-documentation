# lat\_sed

**Code reference:** `hyd_db(ithyd)%lat_sed`

**Declared type:** `hydrology_db` ([reader](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/hydrol_read.f90#L47), [declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/hydrology_data_module.f90#L9)).

Sediment concentration in lateral and tile flow is usually low and does not contribute substantially to total sediment yield unless lateral or tile flow is high.
{% endhint %}
