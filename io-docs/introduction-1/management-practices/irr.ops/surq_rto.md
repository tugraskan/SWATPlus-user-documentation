# sumq\_frac

**Code reference:** `irrop_db(irr_op)%surq`

**Declared type:** `irrigation_operation` ([reader](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/mgt_read_irrops.f90#L48), [declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/mgt_operations_module.f90#L9)).


Dimensionless fraction used to divide irrigation water between water applied to the land and surface runoff. Enter a value from 0.0 to 1.0.
