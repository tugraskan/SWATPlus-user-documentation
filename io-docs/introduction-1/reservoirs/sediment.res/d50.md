# d50

**Code reference:** `res_sed(ires)%d50` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/reservoir_data_module.f90#L101)).

Median particle diameter of suspended and benthic sediment (`µm`; historical table default: `10.0`; Fortran struct default: `0.`). SWAT+ uses `d50` to compute the daily sediment settling factor in [`res_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_read.f90#L143) for reservoirs, [`wet_initial.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wet_initial.f90#L100) for wetlands, and [`cal_parm_select.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cal_parm_select.f90#L836) during calibration, and applies it in [`res_sediment.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_sediment.f90#L41):

$$c_{\text{stl}} = \exp(-0.184 \, d_{50})$$

where $c_{\text{stl}}$ is the daily sediment settling factor (dimensionless) and $d_{50}$ is `d50` (µm).

For impoundments located within a landscape unit, SWAT+ estimates median sediment particle diameter using:

$$d_{50} = \exp\!\left(0.41 \cdot \frac{m_c}{100} + 2.71 \cdot \frac{m_{\text{silt}}}{100} + 5.7 \cdot \frac{m_s}{100}\right)$$

where $d_{50}$ is median sediment particle size (µm), $m_c$ is percent clay in the surface soil layer, $m_{\text{silt}}$ is percent silt, and $m_s$ is percent sand. Because reservoirs receive sediment from the entire upstream drainage area rather than a single HRU, set `d50` to a representative upstream value.
