# lag

**Code reference:** `osrc_om(iom_osrc)%lag` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hydrograph_module.f90#L47), [read](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/om_osrc_read.f90#L43)).

Large-aggregate concentration (`ppm`, equivalent to `mg/L` or `g/m^3`; Fortran struct default: `0.`). Converted to metric tons (`t`) in [`hydrograph_module.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hydrograph_module.f90#L1256):

$$M = \frac{C \cdot Q_{\text{out}}}{10^{6}}$$

where $C$ is `lag` (`ppm`), $Q_{\text{out}}$ is `outflo_om%flo` (`m^3`), and $M$ is the converted mass (`t`). Although [`wallo_start_day.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wallo_start_day.f90#L59) copies `osrc_om(iom)` into `outflo_om`, [`wallo_withdraw.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wallo_withdraw.f90#L40) resets `outflo_om = hz` before [`wallo_withdraw.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/wallo_withdraw.f90#L54-L56), so non-flow components are zeroed prior to conversion in `6f515368`.
