# SD Channel General Output

The individual `channel_sd_{day,mon,yr,aa}` TXT and CSV files use this 66-column schema. Select periods with `channel_sd` in [print.prt](../../introduction-1/simulation-settings/print.prt/README.md) and use `csvout = y` for CSV copies. For the 65-column basin reports, see [Basin SD Channel General Output](basin-sd-channel-general.md). [Individual opener](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_sd_channel.f90#L26)

Daily inlet and outlet flow fields are discharge rates in m3/s. Monthly and yearly values divide the sum of daily rates by the calendar month length or `time%day_end_yr`. Average annual divides the sum of yearly mean rates by `time%yrs_prt`. Precipitation, evaporation, seepage, and water storage are volumes in m3. The storage block uses the current channel state at all intervals. [Writer and period treatment](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_output.f90#L30)

A final partial month contributes no values to the yearly and average-annual inflow, outflow, and water-budget accumulators. Yearly rates and area still use the final day-of-year divisor, and the storage block still uses the state on the last simulated day. [Month transfer and year division](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_output.f90#L45-L85), [annual writes](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_output.f90#L87-L115).

Monthly and yearly `area` divide the sum of daily areas by the same day counts. The average-annual writer applies two year-count divisions to area, so the reported value is the sum of daily areas divided by `time%yrs_prt` squared. Precipitation, evaporation, and seepage are divided by the year count once. [Average-annual water balance](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_output.f90#L84), [Water-body division operators](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/water_body_module.f90#L73)

## Where the daily water values come from

- **Inflow.** The normal controller records the sum of routed inflows, qualifying water transfers, and any separately linked 1-D aquifer inflow. It takes this snapshot before adding channel precipitation or handling overbank flow. The GWFlow channel exchange, canal, tile, and saturation-excess routines change channel storage directly, so those changes do not enter the recorded inlet rate. [Inflow snapshot](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L61-L158) · [Channel exchange](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_channel_exch.f90#L94-L111) · [Canal seepage](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_canal.f90#L140-L147) · [Tile drainage](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_tile.f90#L59-L65) · [Saturation excess](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_satexcess.f90#L56-L61)
- **Area and precipitation.** The sediment routine clears daily precipitation first. When inlet volume exceeds `1.e-6` m³, it computes area as channel length (km) times width (m) divided by 10. It adds rainfall as `10 × depth (mm) × area (ha)` in m³. Otherwise precipitation remains zero and area retains its previous value or initial default. [Area and rainfall assignments](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_sediment3.f90#L72-L106)
- **Evaporation and seepage.** The router computes evaporation from PET, the reach evaporation coefficient, and the length-times-width footprint, capped at available channel storage. It calculates transmission loss using channel width and subtracts it from channel storage. When GWFlow is enabled, the writer's seepage value is forced to zero after that subtraction. [Transmission loss and evaporation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_rtmusk.f90#L192-L225)
- **Storage and outflow.** The printed storage is the in-channel part after the router separates floodplain storage and applies evaporation and transmission loss. The printed outflow uses the routed hydrograph after water-allocation withdrawals. GWFlow updates `ch_stor` directly before routing. The router starts from the separate `tot_stor` state, so it can overwrite those updates when it partitions storage during routing. [Routing and storage partition](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_rtmusk.f90#L109-L187) · [Allocation and outflow assignment](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L330-L372)
- **Near-zero-length bypass.** At channel lengths of `1.e-3` km or less, routing passes the full summed inlet downstream. The printed `flo_in` and `flo_out` instead use the temporary hydrograph left by the last incoming contribution. For two incoming contributions of 100 and 50 m³/day, in that order, this branch routes 150 m³/day and prints `50 / 86400` m³/s in both columns. [Inlet summation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/command.f90#L184-L188) · [Bypass branch](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/command.f90#L374-L389)

## Where the sediment and nutrient values come from

The inlet and outlet blocks report masses. Bulk sediment and the six size classes are in tons. The nine water-quality fields are in kg. Monthly and yearly values sum the daily masses, and average annual scales the accumulated mass by `time%yrs_prt`. The storage block always uses the current in-channel mass at the reporting boundary. [Initial concentration-to-mass conversion](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hydrograph_module.f90#L1236-L1258) · [Period writes](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_output.f90#L44-L115)

- **Inlet and storage.** The inlet snapshot includes routed masses and any water-transfer or linked aquifer masses that enter the hydrograph. It comes before floodplain deposition, overbank transfers, bank erosion, and in-channel deposition. The router adds incoming mass to its channel-plus-floodplain storage, splits the remaining mass between those stores by water volume, and uses only the channel portion for the storage columns. [Inlet snapshot](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L61-L170) · [Routing and storage partition](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_rtmusk.f90#L109-L187)
- **GWFlow nutrients.** When solute transport is enabled, channel exchange and tile drainage can put nitrate and soluble phosphorus directly into channel storage. Channel seepage and canal withdrawals can take them out. The inlet columns come from the separate hydrograph, and routing can replace those storage changes when it partitions the unsynchronized total storage. [Channel exchange additions](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_channel_exch.f90#L158-L164) · [Channel exchange removals](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_channel_exch.f90#L210-L218) · [Tile drainage](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_tile.f90#L95-L98) · [Canal withdrawals](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_canal.f90#L204-L212)
- **Bulk sediment and size classes.** The routed bulk sediment includes bank erosion and reflects deductions for floodplain and channel deposition. The bed erosion calculation is reported in the sediment budget, but the addition to the routed hydrograph is commented out. The sediment routine makes those changes to bulk `sed` without distributing them into `san`, `sil`, `cla`, `sag`, `lag`, or `grv`. Those columns follow the incoming and stored particle-class values through the router, so their sum can differ from bulk sediment. [Floodplain deposition](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_sediment3.f90#L108-L162) · [Bank and bed sediment](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_sediment3.f90#L238-L296) · [Hydrograph scaling](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hydrograph_module.f90#L1440-L1465)
- **Outlet quality.** When inlet volume exceeds `1.e-6` m³, the controller calls the water-quality routine after water routing. With positive rating-curve depth, the routine replaces `orgn`, `sedp`, `no3`, `solp`, `chla`, `nh3`, `no2`, `cbod`, and `dox` in the outlet hydrograph. It converts concentrations back to mass using the adjusted inlet volume `ht1%flo`. On SWAT+ dev the routine also treats inflow at or below `1.e-6` m³ as no flow. If the calculated depth is zero or negative, it clears the entire outlet hydrograph and channel storage. If `qual2e = 1`, the controller then rewrites the first four masses from the adjusted inlet, transferring nitrate to organic N and soluble P to sediment P. [Depth guard](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_watqual4.f90#L97-L134) · [Mass assignments and zero-depth branch](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_watqual4.f90#L332-L346) · [Controller overrides](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L192-L220)
- **Allocation withdrawals.** The channel withdrawal branch copies a fraction of the full outlet hydrograph into the delivered record and reduces only the downstream water volume. For a 25% withdrawal, the delivered record receives 25% of the sediment and nutrient masses and the downstream record still carries the original masses. The individual and basin output files inherit that behavior. [Withdrawal branch](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wallo_withdraw.f90#L72-L85) · [Outlet assignment](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L330-L372)
