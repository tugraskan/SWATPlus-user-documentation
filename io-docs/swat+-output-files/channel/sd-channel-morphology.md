## How to read the reporting periods

The yearly and average-annual accumulators omit a final partial month. The geometry columns use the last month that reached calendar month end in the final simulation year. If no month reached that boundary in that year, the yearly geometry remains at its zero default, which the addition operator carries into the average-annual row. [Per-channel accumulation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_chanmorph_output.f90#L29-L66), [basin accumulation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/basin_chanmorph_output.f90#L33-L69), [geometry copy](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_module.f90#L818-L834), [yearly reset](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/time_control.f90#L313-L319).

In every basin row, `unit` and `gis_id` are both `1`, and `name` is the basin name. The basin operator sums most fields across SWAT-DEG channels. For `width`, `depth`, and `slope`, it takes the values from the last channel in the loop. Through time, both writers retain the most recent values of those three fields. [Basin accumulation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/basin_chanmorph_output.f90#L12), [Field operators](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_module.f90#L818)

| Fields | Per-channel monthly and yearly | Basin monthly and yearly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flow rates, `peakr`, `sed_stor`, `n_tot`, `p_tot`, `dep_bf`, `velav_bf` | Divided by calendar month length or `time%day_end_yr` | Sums across days for both periods |
| Sediment fluxes, erosion distances, and flow depths in mm | Sums | Monthly sums divided by calendar month length. Yearly sums are printed in full. |
| `width`, `depth`, `slope` | Latest channel value | Latest value from the last channel in the basin loop |

For the per-channel average-annual row, the first group is divided by `time%days_prt` and the second by `time%yrs_prt`. For the basin row, the first group is divided by `time%yrs_prt`. The second group is divided by `time%day_end_yr` in the running accumulator after each yearly addition, and then again by `time%days_prt` at the end of the simulation. These basin values follow the source calculations and can differ from a conventional average annual.
