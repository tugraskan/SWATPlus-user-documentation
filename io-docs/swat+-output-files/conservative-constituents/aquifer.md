# Aquifer

Use `aqu_cs` in [print.prt](../../introduction-1/simulation-settings/print.prt/README.md) to select daily, monthly, yearly, or average annual. The filenames start with `aquifer_cs_`. Set `csvout = y` for CSV copies. The opener requires at least one aquifer and a positive configured constituent count. Daily rows follow the daily print window and interval. [File creation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_const.f90#L350), [Daily write](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/aqu_cs_output.f90#L36)

The six identifiers are `jday`, `mon`, `day`, `yr`, `unit` (aquifer index), and `gis_id`. The header always contains 36 columns. The writer supplies ten groups of values in configured constituent order, giving `6 + 10 × constituent count` data values. The fixed headings match the data when the configured list is selenate, selenite, and boron in that order. CSV preambles and headings follow the title. [Fixed header](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_aquifer.f90#L37), [Constituent list reader](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/constit_db_read.f90#L61), [Grouped write list](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/aqu_cs_output.f90#L38)

The `gw_*` fields are discharge fluxes from aquifer to stream. The `mass_*` fields are stored mass. Daily fluxes and stored masses are in kg. Concentration is in g/m3, equivalent to mg/L. Monthly and yearly fluxes are totals. The three state groups (`mass_*`, `conc_*`, `srbd_*`) divide their daily sums by calendar month length or `time%day_end_yr`. In the average-annual row, all ten groups divide their unnormalized sums by the configured simulation year count (`time%nbyr`). The three state groups are therefore sums of daily values per configured year. [Preamble and units](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_const.f90#L363), [Monthly state divisors](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/aqu_cs_output.f90#L68), [Yearly state divisors](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/aqu_cs_output.f90#L130), [Average-annual calculation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/aqu_cs_output.f90#L191)

The writer transfers monthly sums into the yearly record only at calendar month end. If the simulation ends midmonth, the yearly and average-annual sums omit that final partial month. Any yearly state means still use `time%day_end_yr` as the divisor. [Month-end transfer and year-end processing](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/aqu_cs_output.f90#L66-L129)

The `aquifer_cs_*` files contain groups for constituent mass exported with groundwater discharge (`gw_seo4`, `gw_seo3`, `gw_born`), recharge (`rchg_seo4`, `rchg_seo3`, `rchg_born`), seepage, irrigation, diversion, sorption, reaction, mass, concentration, and sorbed mass. The exact remaining headings are `seep_seo4`, `seep_seo3`, `seep_born`; `irr_seo4`, `irr_seo3`, `irr_born`; `div_seo4`, `div_seo3`, `div_born`; `sorb_seo4`, `sorb_seo3`, `sorb_born`; `rctn_seo4`, `rctn_seo3`, `rctn_born`; `mass_seo4`, `mass_seo3`, `mass_born`; `conc_seo4`, `conc_seo3`, `conc_born`; and `srbd_seo4`, `srbd_seo3`, `srbd_born`.

## Where the daily values come from

The 1-D aquifer controller supplies these diagnostics. GWFlow runs through a separate command, while the reporting loop can write rows for all aquifer objects. File creation alone does not establish that a row represents a calculated GWFlow balance. [Model dispatch](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/command.f90#L293-L303) · [Aquifer output loop](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/command.f90#L561-L568)

| Group | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| `rchg` | The controller adds the incoming constituent hydrograph mass to dissolved storage and copies that mass to recharge. [Recharge](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/aqu_1d_control.f90#L225-L231) |
| `gw`, `seep` | The controller calculates concentration from dissolved mass after recharge and the water storage left after the daily water-budget steps. It uses that concentration for both discharge and seepage. Discharge is capped at available constituent mass first, then seepage is capped at the remaining mass. The two loads are assigned to hydrograph slots 1 and 2. [Water-budget steps](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/aqu_1d_control.f90#L97-L136) · [Constituent outflows](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/aqu_1d_control.f90#L232-L262) |
| `mass`, `conc` | After subtracting those two loads, the controller writes remaining dissolved mass and recalculates concentration with the same water volume. Concentration is zero when water volume is zero or negative. [Final dissolved state](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/aqu_1d_control.f90#L263-L270) |
| `irr`, `div` | The aquifer irrigation assignment is in `cs_irrig`, whose call is commented out. The inspected source has no active producer for the diversion diagnostic. These slots stay at their defaults on the active path. [Irrigation assignment](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_irrig.f90#L99-L105) · [Disabled call](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/wallo_pou_deliv.f90#L61-L68) · [Daily reset](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L627-L634) |
| `rctn` | Before the day's recharge and flow calculations, the reaction routine records dissolved mass after minus before the reaction for selenate and selenite. There is no boron reaction assignment. [Controller order](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/aqu_1d_control.f90#L85-L98) · [Reaction assignment](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_rctn_aqu.f90#L97-L107) |
| `sorb`, `srbd` | After reaction and before recharge, sorption records the decrease in dissolved mass for all three constituents. A positive value means net transfer out of solution. The sorbed-state group stores whole-aquifer mass in kg. The sorption calculation uses the original database bottom depth and the object's current specific yield to calculate aquifer material volume. [Controller order](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/aqu_1d_control.f90#L85-L98) · [Material volume](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_sorb_aqu.f90#L54-L62) · [Sorption and sorbed mass](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_sorb_aqu.f90#L181-L200) |
