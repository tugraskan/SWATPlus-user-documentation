# Basin balance

The four `basin_cs_*` reports are TXT files. All have 90 columns, starting with `yr`, `mo`, and `jday`, followed by three fixed 29-field blocks. The calculation indexes constituents 1, 2, and 3 directly as selenate, selenite, and boron. Configure that order for the labels to match the records. [File openers](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_const.f90#L16), [Fixed headings](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_module.f90#L79), [Basin indexing](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L37)

The `basin_cs` row in [print.prt](../../introduction-1/simulation-settings/print.prt/README.md) controls opening the named files. The writer still writes to the daily unit every reporting day and to the other units at period boundaries, without checking those switches or the daily print interval. Select all four periods to have all four named files opened when this constituent balance runs. With a period disabled, the writer still targets that unit. The resulting runtime behavior has not been checked. [Period openers](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_const.f90#L16), [Reporting caller](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/command.f90#L651), [Daily and period writes](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L501)

## Where the daily values come from

Each constituent block has the same 29 quantities. The report tabulates transfers and selected stores. It has no whole-system residual or channel, wetland, or reservoir storage column. [Daily writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L501-L510)

| Headings before the constituent suffix | Daily calculation in kg |
| --- | --- |
| `latq`, `surq`, `sedm`, `urbq`, `wetq`, `perc`, `gwup`, `wtsp`, `irsw`, `irgw`, `irwo`, `rain`, `dryd`, `fert`, `uptk`, `rct_sl`, `srb_sl` | Sums the corresponding diagnostics from full HRUs. Each value in kg/ha is multiplied by its HRU area in hectares. The [HRU calculation notes](hru.md#where-the-daily-values-come-from) describe the inactive diagnostics and the difference between groundwater transfer and wetland seepage. [Runoff sums](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L38-L101) · [Other HRU transfers](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L126-L280) |
| `tile` | Adds HRU tile mass to the GWFlow cell tile term when GWFlow solute transport is enabled. The cell term is multiplied by `-1 / 1000` to convert grams leaving groundwater to positive kilograms in the basin report. [Tile sum](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L103-L124) |
| `ptso`, `ptout` | Sum the Recall mass records classified as within the watershed and from outside the watershed, respectively. The reader assigns a title value of `Incoming` to the outside group. Both terms are inputs. A negative daily Recall diversion removes channel storage directly and leaves the Recall constituent hydrograph at zero for that record. [Classification](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/recall_read_cs.f90#L113-L126) · [Recall producer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/recall_cs.f90#L25-L57) · [Point-source sums](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L282-L306) |
| `dis_sl`, `sbd_sl` | Sum dissolved and sorbed mass over every soil layer of each full HRU, multiplying each term by HRU area. [Soil stores](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L308-L336) |
| `gw` | Sums discharge mass from 1-D aquifers when GWFlow is off. With GWFlow solute transport enabled, it reports `-(gwsw + swgw + satx) / 1000` summed across cells. Groundwater discharge and saturation excess add to this value, and channel leakage into groundwater reduces it. [Groundwater sum](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L341-L372) · [Groundwater discharge](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_channel_exch.f90#L152-L158) · [Channel leakage](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_channel_exch.f90#L204-L242) · [Saturation excess](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_satexcess.f90#L90-L102) |
| `rchg`, `rct_aq`, `srb_aq` | Sum the 1-D aquifer recharge, reaction, and sorption diagnostics when GWFlow is off. With GWFlow solute transport enabled, the report sums cell `rech`, signed `rcti + rcto`, and `sorb`, respectively, converting g to kg. [Recharge](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L374-L396) · [Reaction and sorption](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L411-L457) · [GWFlow reaction signs](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_solute.f90#L134-L149) |
| `seep` | Always sums the 1-D aquifer seepage diagnostic. [Aquifer seepage](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L398-L409) |
| `dis_aq`, `sbd_aq` | Dissolved mass is the sum of 1-D aquifer mass, or the sum of GWFlow cell mass for cells with status greater than zero when GWFlow solute transport is enabled. Sorbed mass always sums the 1-D aquifer pool in kg/ha times each aquifer object's area. [Dissolved groundwater mass](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L459-L483) · [Sorbed groundwater mass](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L485-L498) |

With GWFlow active and solute transport disabled, `gw`, `rchg`, `rct_aq`, `srb_aq`, and `dis_aq` stay at zero. With solute transport enabled, the basin routine reads three fixed slots immediately after nitrate, phosphorus, and the configured salts. [Groundwater branches](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L341-L483) · [GWFlow solute ordering](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_read.f90#L1609-L1638)

The GWFlow command clears the daily cell `gwsw`, `swgw`, `satx`, and `tile` terms before the basin report runs. Only contributions supplied after that reset can appear in the basin `gw` and GWFlow portion of `tile`. The cell recharge, reaction, and sorption terms are kept until the basin report reads and clears them. [GWFlow reset](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_simulate.f90#L552-L572) · [Command and report order](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/command.f90#L293-L298) · [Basin report call](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/command.f90#L647-L653) · [Basin reset](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L664-L679)

## How to read the reporting periods

The HRU fertilizer and plant-uptake inputs can carry over from the skipped years into the first reporting day. The basin balance sums those HRU diagnostics before clearing them. [HRU diagnostic sources and warm-up behavior](hru.md#where-the-daily-values-come-from)

Monthly and yearly fluxes are sums in kg. The four storage fields in each constituent block (`dis_sl`, `sbd_sl`, `dis_aq`, `sbd_aq`) divide daily sums by day of month at month end or by `time%day_end_yr` at year end. The basin calculation converts HRU kg/ha fluxes such as `latq` to kg using HRU area before summing. [HRU-area conversion](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L37), [Monthly and yearly normalization](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L513)

The writer adds all 87 daily values directly to its yearly and average-annual accumulators. Both annual results include a final partial month. A monthly row requires calendar month end. [Daily accumulation and period writes](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L506-L554)

The average-annual writer divides the flux slots by the configured simulation year count (`time%nbyr`), then applies a second division by `time%days_prt` to the following fields. It also divides the two storage fields shown below by `time%days_prt`. The other storage fields are left as sums of daily masses even though the preamble describes averaged states. [Average-annual divisions](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L553), [Printed preamble](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_const.f90#L173)

| Constituent | Fluxes divided by both years and days | Storage divided by days |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selenate | `ptout_seo4`, `srb_aq_seo4` | `dis_sl_seo4`, `dis_aq_seo4` |
| Selenite | `ptso_seo3`, `ptout_seo3`, `rct_aq_seo3`, `srb_aq_seo3` | None |
| Boron | `srb_sl_born`, `ptso_born`, `seep_born`, `rct_aq_born` | None |
