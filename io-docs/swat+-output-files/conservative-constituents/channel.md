# Channel

`channel_cs_day`, `channel_cs_mon`, `channel_cs_yr`, and `channel_cs_aa` use the `channel_cs` row in [print.prt](../../introduction-1/simulation-settings/print.prt/README.md). Set `csvout = y` for CSV copies. The opener requires a SWAT-DEG channel and a positive constituent count. After the skipped years, the daily switch writes on each reporting day without a separate daily print-window check. [File opener](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_const.f90#L479) · [Daily writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_cs_output.f90#L29) · [Reporting call](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/command.f90#L578)

The header has 30 columns. After `jday`, `mon`, `day`, `yr`, `unit` (channel index), and `gis_id`, the writer supplies eight groups in configured constituent order. Each row contains `6 + 8 × constituent count` values. The fixed headings match a configured list of selenate, selenite, and boron in that order. [Header declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_cs_module.f90#L29) · [Write list](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_cs_output.f90#L42)

Monthly and yearly fluxes are totals. Stored mass and concentration are means of daily values using calendar month length or `time%day_end_yr`. For average annual, all eight groups divide the unaveraged sums by `time%nbyr`. Stored mass and concentration in that row are therefore sums of daily values per configured year. The TXT preamble labels average-annual fluxes kg/yr and retains kg and g/m³ for mass and concentration. [Monthly and yearly calculations](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_cs_output.f90#L66) · [Average-annual calculation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_cs_output.f90#L173) · [TXT preamble](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_const.f90#L570)

The writer transfers monthly sums into the yearly record only at calendar month end. If the simulation ends midmonth, the yearly and average-annual sums omit that final partial month. Any yearly state means still use `time%day_end_yr` as the divisor. [Month-end transfer and year-end processing](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_cs_output.f90#L66-L120)

## Where the daily values come from

| Group | Calculation and limits |
| --- | --- |
| `in`, `gw` | The controller records the linked 1-D aquifer load times its channel fraction in `gw`. It adds linked-aquifer and water-transfer constituents to its working inlet, then replaces that inlet with the original routed hydrograph before calculating constituent storage and outflow. The reported `in` adds the saved `gw` diagnostic to the original routed inlet. GWFlow channel exchange updates channel constituent storage directly. [Water-transfer addition](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L80-L90) · [Linked aquifer and inlet replacement](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L117-L175) · [Reported inlet](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L482-L485) · [GWFlow storage update](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_channel_exch.f90#L172-L178) |
| `seep` | The controller calculates a concentration from the routed inlet mass and the initial routed outlet volume. It multiplies that concentration by the router's seepage volume, caps the result at available inlet mass, and deducts it before mixing with stored mass. The branch runs only when the adjusted inlet water volume exceeds `1.e-6` m³. On days that skip it, the seepage diagnostic retains its previous value and can be added again to longer-period totals. With GWFlow enabled, the router supplies zero seepage volume to this calculation. [Initial concentration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L173-L193) · [Seepage calculation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L233-L242) · [Daily reset list](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_balance.f90#L655-L662) · [GWFlow seepage flag](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_rtmusk.f90#L192-L208) |
| `out`, `mass` | The wet-inlet branch forms a mixture of the inlet mass remaining after seepage and the stored channel mass. Later the controller routes `scoef × mixture` downstream and stores the remainder. The controller's coefficient is initialized to zero and assigned only in the branch where travel time exceeds the routing step. Shorter travel-time calls can reuse the coefficient from an earlier call. If the wet-inlet branch is skipped, the mixture remains at its zero default for that object, and the later partition can clear the stored mass. [Scratch reset](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/command.f90#L97-L105) · [Mixing and coefficient](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L264-L275) · [Partition and downstream assignment](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L348-L368) |
| `conc` | The writer uses final constituent outflow mass times 1,000 divided by the final outlet water volume, after any water-allocation withdrawal. A zero or negative outlet volume gives zero concentration. The unit is g/m³, numerically equivalent to mg/L. [Allocation and final water volume](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L330-L338) · [Reported concentration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L482-L491) |
| `irr` | The only assignment that adds an irrigation mass is in `cs_irrig`, whose call is commented out in water allocation. [Irrigation assignment](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cs_irrig.f90#L135-L141) · [Disabled call](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/wallo_pou_deliv.f90#L61-L68) |
| `div` | A negative daily Recall flow removes constituent mass directly from the first linked channel's storage when that channel has more than 10 m³ of water. The routine caps removal at available mass and assigns the negative mass to `div`. A second qualifying diversion on the same day replaces the first diagnostic value. Positive loads enter the Recall hydrograph. [Recall constituent dispatch](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/command.f90#L352-L356) · [Diversion and point-source assignments](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/recall_cs.f90#L30-L51) |

For channels with length at or below `1.e-3` km, the bypass copies the incoming constituent hydrograph downstream without running the controller that fills these daily fields. [Channel bypass](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/command.f90#L374-L407)

## Column order

The `channel_cs_*` files contain these exact groups: `seo4_in`, `seo3_in`, `born_in`; `seo4_gw`, `seo3_gw`, `born_gw`; `seo4_out`, `seo3_out`, `born_out`; `seo4_seep`, `seo3_seep`, `born_seep`; `seo4_irr`, `seo3_irr`, `born_irr`; `seo4_div`, `seo3_div`, `born_div`; `seo4_mass`, `seo3_mass`, `born_mass`; and `seo4_conc`, `seo3_conc`, `born_conc`. Masses are kg and concentrations are g/m3.
