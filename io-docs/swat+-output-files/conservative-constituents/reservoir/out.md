**Printed header value:** `seo4_out`, `seo3_out`, `born_out` ([header names](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_cs_module.f90#L67-L69)). The legend line reads `out:         kg       mass leaving reservoir with outflow` ([legend](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_const.f90#L608)).
