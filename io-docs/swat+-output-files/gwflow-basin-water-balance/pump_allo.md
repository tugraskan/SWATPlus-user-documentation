# pump_allo

**Code reference:** `ppag_grid` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_output.f90#L591)); `gw_hyd_grid_mo%ppag` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_module.f90#L128)).

Groundwater pumping diagnostic from the HRU-irrigation allocation path.

**Unit:** mm.

The daily diagnostic is summed over active cells and divided by the full basin area, then converted from m3 to mm. Monthly and yearly basin values sum these normalized daily diagnostics. Average-annual output divides the simulation accumulator by `real(time%nbyr)`, the configured simulation-year count, rather than `time%yrs_prt` or a separately counted set of reporting years.

The active HRU-irrigation allocation path records removed water with a negative sign. Its daily per-cell field is assigned, not accumulated: repeated pumping requests for the same cell overwrite the previous diagnostic. Basin period totals sum the resulting daily diagnostics, not necessarily every individual pumping event. ([producer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_pump_allo.f90#L81)).

The basin accumulators are updated independently of whether daily output is enabled ([normalization and accumulation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_output.f90#L869); [average-annual divisor](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_output.f90#L1985)).
