# pump_def

**Code reference:** `ppdf_grid` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_output.f90#L592)); `gw_hyd_grid_mo%ppdf` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_module.f90#L129)).

Groundwater pumping demand that could not be met. It is reported separately from the amount actually pumped and should not be counted as an additional withdrawal.

**Unit:** mm.

The daily diagnostic is summed over active cells and divided by the full basin area, then converted from m3 to mm. Monthly and yearly basin values sum these normalized daily diagnostics. Average-annual output divides the simulation accumulator by `real(time%nbyr)`, the configured simulation-year count, rather than `time%yrs_prt` or a separately counted set of reporting years.

This is nonnegative unmet demand, not an actual withdrawal. The daily per-cell deficit is assigned, not accumulated; repeated requests replace its previous diagnostic. It is excluded from the water-balance error's signed flux sum. ([producer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_pump_allo.f90#L100)).

The basin accumulators are updated independently of whether daily output is enabled ([normalization and accumulation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_output.f90#L869); [average-annual divisor](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_output.f90#L1985)).
