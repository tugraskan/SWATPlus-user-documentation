# soil

**Code reference:** `soil_grid` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_output.f90#L588)); `gw_hyd_grid_mo%soil` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_module.f90#L124)).

Groundwater transferred upward into the soil profile.

**Unit:** mm.

The daily diagnostic is summed over active cells and divided by the full basin area, then converted from m3 to mm. Monthly and yearly basin values sum these normalized daily diagnostics. Average-annual output divides the simulation accumulator by `real(time%nbyr)`, the configured simulation-year count, rather than `time%yrs_prt` or a separately counted set of reporting years.

Negative values represent groundwater transferred into the soil profile. ([producer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_soil.f90#L67)).

The transfer requires an active cell with a water table inside the HRU soil profile. The routine multiplies the saturated thickness within the profile by the overlapping cell area and the cell's specific yield. The resulting volume is recorded as a groundwater loss and apportioned among the receiving soil layers by saturated thickness. [Volume calculation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_soil.f90#L40-L69) · [Layer allocation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_soil.f90#L81-L109)

The basin accumulators are updated independently of whether daily output is enabled ([normalization and accumulation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_output.f90#L869); [average-annual divisor](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_output.f90#L1985)).
