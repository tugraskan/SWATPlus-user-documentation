# vaft

**Code reference:** `vaft_grid` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_module.f90#L155)).

Groundwater storage after the daily balance, summed across active groundwater cells and converted to an equivalent depth over the total basin area.

**Unit:** mm.

This field appears in the daily file only; it is not a column in the monthly, yearly, or average-annual basin water-balance files.
