# wetland

**Code reference:** `wetl_grid` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_output.f90#L596)); `gw_hyd_grid_mo%wetl` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_module.f90#L133)).

Groundwater entering wetlands from the aquifer, reported as a negative removal.

**Unit:** mm.

The daily diagnostic is summed over active cells and divided by the full basin area, then converted from m3 to mm. Monthly and yearly basin values sum these normalized daily diagnostics. Average-annual output divides the simulation accumulator by `real(time%nbyr)`, the configured simulation-year count, rather than `time%yrs_prt` or a separately counted set of reporting years.

The inspected wetland producer records only groundwater entering wetlands, with a negative sign. This component is not a two-way net exchange with wetlands. ([producer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_wetland.f90#L68)).

Groundwater enters the wetland when groundwater head exceeds the wetland stage, which is HRU elevation plus ponded depth. The routine uses the head difference, bed conductivity, bed thickness, and the wetland area assigned to the connection, capped against available groundwater. In the other direction, the routine accumulates a separate wetland-to-soil seepage volume. The wetland controller can reduce that volume further for soil saturation before subtracting it from wetland storage.
