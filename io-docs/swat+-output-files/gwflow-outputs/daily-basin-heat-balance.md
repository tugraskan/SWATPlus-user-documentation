# Daily GWFlow Basin Heat Balance

`gwflow_basin_heat_day.txt` has 29 headings/data values: `jday`, `mon`, `day`, `yr`, `unit`, `gis_id`, `name`, `ts`, `hbef`, `haft`, `recharge`, `gw_et`, `gw_sw`, `sw_gw`, `sat_excess`, `soil`, `lateral`, `dispersion`, `boundary`, `pump_allo`, `pump_ext`, `tile`, `reservoir`, `wetland`, `canal`, `floodplain`, `pond`, `error`, and `tavg`. Heat terms are MJ, `error` is percent, and `tavg` is degC. Set `heat = 1` in `codes.gw` and enable daily `gwflow_heat`. If the print row is absent, daily heat output follows the effective daily water-balance flag.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The file has 29 headings/data values but only 28 unit entries. The source prints `%` under `pond`, `degC` under `error`, and no unit under `tavg`. Their actual units are MJ, percent, and °C, respectively. [Header and units](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_output.f90#L190-L201)
{% endhint %}

Only interior cells (`status == 1`) contribute to the basin sums. The writer divides their heat amounts in joules by 1,000,000 to obtain MJ. `ts` is the groundwater solver step in days. [Basin sum and daily row](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_output.f90#L983-L1041)

## Heat storage and flux fields

| Column | Value written |
| --- | --- |
| `hbef` | Sum of beginning-of-day heat, calculated from cell temperature × water density × specific heat × beginning water volume. Cells with nonpositive beginning volume contribute zero. [Beginning checkpoint](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_simulate.f90#L307-L326) |
| `haft` | Sum of cell heat storage after the heat solver. [Ending checkpoint](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_simulate.f90#L439-L446) |
| `recharge` | Heat from deep percolation calculated using bottom soil-layer temperature. The code blends today's heat with the previous delayed heat and distributes it using HRU or LSU cell fractions. [Delay](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_rech.f90#L44-L56), [LSU transfer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_rech.f90#L88-L105), [HRU transfer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_rech.f90#L124-L140) |
| `gw_et` | Cell temperature × water density × specific heat × accumulated signed groundwater ET volume. [Cell ET](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_gwet.f90#L65-L80), [Other connection branch](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_gwet.f90#L112-L126) |
| `gw_sw`, `sw_gw` | Separate groundwater-to-channel and channel-to-groundwater heat terms. The channel-to-groundwater branch uses positive channel temperature and limits the transfer to the channel's available heat. [Channel exchange](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_channel_exch.f90#L113-L145) |
| `sat_excess`, `soil`, `tile` | Heat removed with saturation-excess water, groundwater transfer to soil, and tile drainage. The daily cell slot is assigned for saturation excess and tile drainage and accumulated for soil transfer. [Saturation excess](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_satexcess.f90#L65-L81), [Soil](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_soil.f90#L67-L79), [Tile](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_tile.f90#L60-L75) |
| `lateral`, `boundary` | Heat advected with lateral water flow using the contributing cell's temperature. Amounts accumulate across solver steps. Transfer with a status-2 neighbor goes into `boundary`, with other neighbors into `lateral`. [Advection and routing to fields](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_heat.f90#L47-L73) |
| `dispersion` | Heat from the intercell temperature gradient, interface conductivity, and cross-section area. The daily slot is overwritten at each solver step and keeps the last step's value, although each step updates heat storage. [Solver loop](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_lateral.f90#L163-L179), [Dispersion and storage update](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_heat.f90#L75-L121) |
| `pump_allo`, `pump_ext` | Heat removed by irrigation allocation and other pumping, respectively. Allocation routines assign the cell diagnostic, so later requests can replace earlier values. The specified pumping routine subtracts its heat from the existing `pump_ext` diagnostic. [Irrigation pump](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_pump_allo.f90#L106-L114), [Other allocation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_pump_allo.f90#L355-L363), [Specified pumping](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_pump_ext.f90#L52-L61) |
| `reservoir`, `wetland` | Heat carried from groundwater into the reservoir or wetland. The reservoir heat branch covers groundwater discharge only. [Reservoir exchange](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_reservoir.f90#L91-L101), [Wetland discharge](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_wetland.f90#L91-L99) |
| `canal`, `floodplain` | Signed heat exchange. Channel-connected canals and floodplains can transfer heat in either direction. The external- and diversion-canal routines add heat terms only for groundwater discharge to the canal. [Channel-connected canal](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_canal.f90#L150-L181), [External canal](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_canal_ext.f90#L91-L101), [Diversion canal](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_canal_div.f90#L123-L133), [Floodplain](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_floodplain.f90#L106-L134) |
| `pond` | The daily heat component stays zero. The pond routine supplies groundwater recharge water and solute mass but never assigns the pond heat component. [Water and solute transfers](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_pond.f90#L240-L320), [Daily heat reset](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_simulate.f90#L507-L524) |

`error` is calculated as 100 × (1 − (beginning heat + all 17 signed heat fluxes) ÷ ending heat). The writer divides by ending heat without checking for zero. With multiple solver steps, the equation uses only the last reported dispersion contribution. [Error calculation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_output.f90#L983-L1010)

`tavg` is the arithmetic mean of interior-cell temperatures after the solve. Each cell has equal weight regardless of area or water storage. The mean has no zero-count guard. [Temperature mean](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_simulate.f90#L406-L417)
