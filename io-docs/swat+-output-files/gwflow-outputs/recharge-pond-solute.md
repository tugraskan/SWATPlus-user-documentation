# GWFlow Recharge-Pond Solute Rows

`gwflow_pond_sol_day.txt` uses the fixed 13-column schema `jday`, `mon`, `day`, `yr`, `unit`, `gis_id`, `name`, `area`, `storage`, `solute`, `mass`, `div_added`, and `recharge`. `area` is m², `storage` is m³, and the final three fields are kg. Under the [pond water-balance settings](recharge-pond-water-balance.md), the writer emits one row per configured solute for each pond that reaches the water-balance write. [Headers and units](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_read.f90#L2024-L2038), [Writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_pond.f90#L333-L358)

`mass` is remaining stored mass and `recharge` is the sum of the day's mass transferred to connected cells. For each active cell with water available in the pond, the code multiplies the concentration before seepage by the water transferred, caps the result at available mass, and removes it from pond storage. [Recharge mass calculations](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_pond.f90#L240-L320)

Channel-source `div_added` includes solute transfer only when the channel volume before withdrawal exceeds 10 m³. The canal-source branch fills the field for salts and conservative constituents. The outside-source branch can add NO₃ and P to stored mass while leaving `div_added` at zero for those two rows. It does fill the field for salts and conservative constituents. [Channel transfers](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_pond.f90#L85-L149), [Canal and outside-source transfers](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_pond.f90#L150-L222)
