# amnd_cl

**Printed heading:** `amnd_cl`, position 140 in the fixed 159-entry header. The mappings below apply to the eight-ion case in the order sulfate, calcium, magnesium, sodium, potassium, chloride, carbonate, bicarbonate. The writer uses `cs_db%num_salts` values per group; for another count, reconstruct the row from the WRITE list.

**Actual value:** `hsaltb_d(j)%salt(6)%uptk` ([daily writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L48); [record declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/salt_module.f90#L7)). Chloride plant-uptake salt diagnostic.

**Unit:** kg/ha.

**Header mismatch:** The nominal heading denotes soil-amendment salt diagnostic, but the value at this position is `%uptk`. The omitted groundwater-upward-transfer group shifts the following values eight places to the left. Do not use the nominal label to assign the actual quantity or unit.

**Calculation**

For each call with positive root depth and root mass, the routine uses the **first plant** in the HRU to choose the daily ion uptake and distribute it across rooted layers. Each layer receives its share of the prescribed kg/ha, capped by the dissolved ion mass available there. The diagnostic accumulates the amount subtracted from soil. The growth routine can call this calculation for several plants on the same day while the calculation continues to use plant slot 1. [Root distribution and removal](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/salt_uptake.f90#L38) · [Uptake guard](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/pl_biomass_gro.f90#L91) · [Plant loop](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/pl_grow.f90#L27)

Monthly and yearly values sum the daily diagnostic. Average annual divides the accumulated yearly diagnostic by configured `time%nbyr`, giving the annual amount convention kg/ha/yr. The denominator is the configured simulation-year count, not a count of years contributing output; skipped or partial periods require care.

The corresponding period members are `hsaltb_m(j)%salt(6)%uptk`, `hsaltb_y(j)%salt(6)%uptk`, and `hsaltb_a(j)%salt(6)%uptk` ([monthly/yearly transfer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L95); [yearly/AA transfer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L191); [AA division](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L288)).

See the [complete schema and period limitations](README.md#actual-data-layout). This page identifies the stored diagnostic and writer aggregation; it does not independently validate every upstream transport or application calculation.
