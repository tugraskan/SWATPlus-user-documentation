# irgw_co3

**Printed heading:** `irgw_co3`, position 93 in the fixed 159-entry header. The mappings below apply to the eight-ion case in the order sulfate, calcium, magnesium, sodium, potassium, chloride, carbonate, bicarbonate. The writer uses `cs_db%num_salts` values per group; for another count, reconstruct the row from the WRITE list.

**Actual value:** `hsaltb_d(j)%salt(7)%irwo` ([daily writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L48); [record declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/salt_module.f90#L7)). Carbonate outside-source irrigation salt diagnostic.

**Unit:** kg/ha.

**Header mismatch:** The nominal heading denotes groundwater irrigation salt diagnostic, but the value at this position is `%irwo`. The omitted groundwater-upward-transfer group shifts the following values eight places to the left. Do not use the nominal label to assign the actual quantity or unit.

**Calculation**

The dormant `salt_irrig` routine calculates outside-source soil delivery as irrigation concentration times water volume divided by 1000 and by HRU area. This converts g/m3 (equivalent to mg/L) and m3 to kg/ha. The water allocation call is commented out in this revision, leaving this field at its initialized or reset value in the traced execution path. [Legacy unlimited-source branch](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/salt_irrig.f90#L181) · [Commented call](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wallo_pou_deliv.f90#L68)

Monthly and yearly values sum the daily diagnostic. Average annual divides the accumulated yearly diagnostic by configured `time%nbyr`, giving the annual amount convention kg/ha/yr. The denominator is the configured simulation-year count, not a count of years contributing output; skipped or partial periods require care.

The corresponding period members are `hsaltb_m(j)%salt(7)%irwo`, `hsaltb_y(j)%salt(7)%irwo`, and `hsaltb_a(j)%salt(7)%irwo` ([monthly/yearly transfer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L95); [yearly/AA transfer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L191); [AA division](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L288)).

See the [complete schema and period limitations](README.md#actual-data-layout). This page identifies the stored diagnostic and writer aggregation; it does not independently validate every upstream transport or application calculation.
