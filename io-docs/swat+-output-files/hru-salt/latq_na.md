# latq_na

**Printed heading:** `latq_na`, position 26 in the fixed 159-entry header. The mappings below apply to the eight-ion case in the order sulfate, calcium, magnesium, sodium, potassium, chloride, carbonate, bicarbonate. The writer uses `cs_db%num_salts` values per group; for another count, reconstruct the row from the WRITE list.

**Actual value:** `hsaltb_d(j)%salt(4)%latq` ([daily writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L48); [record declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/salt_module.f90#L7)). Sodium lateral-flow salt diagnostic.

**Unit:** kg/ha.

**Producer and interpretation:** [Layer lateral-flow calculation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/salt_lch.f90#L142) multiplies each layer's lateral water depth `flat` by its mobile salt concentration, using `cosurfsalt` in the top layer and `cosalt` below it. Each amount is capped by the dissolved mass still in that layer, removed from that layer, and added to `latqsalt(j,4)`. This is the sum of lateral soil losses across layers, not a concentration or a signed groundwater exchange. The HRU hydrograph converts it to kg by multiplying by HRU area ([lateral hydrograph](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_hyds.f90#L121)).

Monthly and yearly values sum the daily diagnostic. Average annual divides the accumulated yearly diagnostic by configured `time%nbyr`, giving the annual amount convention kg/ha/yr. The denominator is the configured simulation-year count, not a count of years contributing output; skipped or partial periods require care.

The corresponding period members are `hsaltb_m(j)%salt(4)%latq`, `hsaltb_y(j)%salt(4)%latq`, and `hsaltb_a(j)%salt(4)%latq` ([monthly/yearly transfer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L95); [yearly/AA transfer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L191); [AA division](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L288)).

See the [complete schema and period limitations](README.md#actual-data-layout). This page identifies the stored diagnostic and writer aggregation; it does not independently validate every upstream transport or application calculation.
