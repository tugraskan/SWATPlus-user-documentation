# rain_k

**Printed heading:** `rain_k`, position 107 in the fixed 159-entry header. The mappings below apply to the eight-ion case in the order sulfate, calcium, magnesium, sodium, potassium, chloride, carbonate, bicarbonate. The writer uses `cs_db%num_salts` values per group; for another count, reconstruct the row from the WRITE list.

**Actual value:** `hsaltb_d(j)%salt(5)%dryd` ([daily writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L48); [record declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/salt_module.f90#L7)). Potassium dry-deposition salt diagnostic.

**Unit:** kg/ha.

**Header mismatch:** The nominal heading denotes rainfall salt diagnostic, but the value at this position is `%dryd`. The omitted groundwater-upward-transfer group shifts the following values eight places to the left. Do not use the nominal label to assign the actual quantity or unit.

**Calculation**

The producer adds dry deposition to the top soil layer even on a day with zero precipitation. It divides the monthly input by the calendar month length and divides yearly or average-annual inputs by 365, including in leap years. Monthly and yearly inputs require a valid deposition series index. The producer runs when salt deposition is enabled and replaces the daily diagnostic. [Daily deposition formulas](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/salt_rain.f90#L42) · [Caller](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_control.f90#L565)

Monthly and yearly values sum the daily diagnostic. Average annual divides the accumulated yearly diagnostic by configured `time%nbyr`, giving the annual amount convention kg/ha/yr. The denominator is the configured simulation-year count, not a count of years contributing output; skipped or partial periods require care.

The corresponding period members are `hsaltb_m(j)%salt(5)%dryd`, `hsaltb_y(j)%salt(5)%dryd`, and `hsaltb_a(j)%salt(5)%dryd` ([monthly/yearly transfer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L95); [yearly/AA transfer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L191); [AA division](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_salt_output.f90#L288)).

See the [complete schema and period limitations](README.md#actual-data-layout). This page identifies the stored diagnostic and writer aggregation; it does not independently validate every upstream transport or application calculation.
