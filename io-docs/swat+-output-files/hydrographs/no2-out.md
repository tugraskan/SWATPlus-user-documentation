# no2

**Code reference:** `ht1%no2` ([daily writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hydout_output.f90#L22); [declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hydrograph_module.f90#L40)).

Outgoing nitrite nitrogen.

**Unit:** kg N.

Daily volume and constituent mass values are added into monthly and yearly totals; the average-annual record divides accumulated volume and masses by `time%yrs_prt`. The [hydrograph operators](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hydrograph_module.f90#L1337) combine temperature by water-volume weighting, and division by years leaves temperature unchanged. The writers perform no volume-to-rate conversion.
