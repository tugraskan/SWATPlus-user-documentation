# Channel and reservoir pesticide balance

The individual `channel_pest_*` and `reservoir_pest_*` reports and their basin counterparts share this 22-column layout. All four periods support TXT and CSV. The opener writes a title and headings, with kg indicated in the mass headings. Select the period on the [shared pesticide print row](README.md#how-to-produce-these-files). [Channel opener](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_pest.f90#L78), [Reservoir opener](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_pest.f90#L142), [Basin openers](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_pest.f90#L334)

The basin writers sum the records across channels or reservoirs for each pesticide. They print `1` in both identifier columns and use the first channel or first reservoir name. The summed input and output fields can include transfers between objects within the basin. [Basin channel writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/basin_ch_pest_output.f90#L23), [Basin reservoir writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/basin_res_pest_output.f90#L23)

## How to read the reporting periods

The source applies different calculations to channel and reservoir records. The rules below apply to both process masses and the two storage columns. [Channel operators](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_pesticide_module.f90#L102), [Reservoir operators](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_pesticide_module.f90#L98)

| Report | Monthly | Yearly | Average annual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Individual and basin channel | Sum of daily values multiplied by calendar month length | Yearly accumulator multiplied by `time%day_end_yr` | Individual channel divides the accumulated record by `time%yrs_prt` and multiplies it by the same count. Basin channel divides by `time%yrs_prt` and multiplies by `time%days_prt`. |
| Individual and basin reservoir | Sum of daily values | Yearly accumulator as stored | Sum of yearly accumulations divided by `time%yrs_prt` |

All four writers transfer monthly sums only at calendar month end. A final partial month is omitted from yearly and average-annual accumulations.

The channel and reservoir writers leave their individual and basin yearly accumulators in place after year end. The next year's completed months are added to the carried values. The channel writer also scales its yearly accumulator in place before carrying it forward. For example, if two complete years contribute reservoir input masses of 2 kg and 3 kg, the second yearly row shows 5 kg. The average-annual numerator receives 2 kg plus 5 kg. [Individual channel year end](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cha_pesticide_output.f90#L67-L87), [basin channel year end](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/basin_ch_pest_output.f90#L69-L89), [individual reservoir year end](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_pesticide_output.f90#L64-L84), [basin reservoir year end](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/basin_res_pest_output.f90#L69-L89).

The writers transfer unscaled monthly and yearly accumulators into the next reporting period before applying the displayed period calculations. In reservoir files, the longer-period storage fields are sums of daily stored masses, with year-count division in the average-annual file. In channel files, storage undergoes the same scaling as the flux columns. Use the daily files to read the stored mass on a particular date. [Individual channel periods](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/cha_pesticide_output.f90#L48), [Basin channel periods](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/basin_ch_pest_output.f90#L50), [Individual reservoir periods](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_pesticide_output.f90#L45), [Basin reservoir periods](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/basin_res_pest_output.f90#L50)

## Daily values for channels

- **Inflow, split, and storage.** The reported inlet is the incoming routed pesticide mass. The routine processes that load with the mass already in channel water and bed sediment. It splits the post-process water mass into routed export and remaining water storage using release volume divided by release plus channel storage volume, with a small denominator offset and an upper bound of 1. The controller then splits that same routed mass between `sol_out_kg` and `sor_out_kg` using a dissolved fraction recalculated for each pesticide. With negligible incoming sediment or water volume, the split reports all of the routed mass as dissolved. [Incoming load and water volume](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_rtpest.f90#L57-L76), [Export and storage](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_rtpest.f90#L239-L251), [Reported split and storage](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L441-L467).
- **Process masses.** The controller copies water reaction, volatilization, settling, resuspension, signed diffusion, bed reaction, and burial from the pesticide routine. Positive diffusion means bed to water and negative means water to bed. Channel water and bed metabolite values are summed separately on the daughter row, and the routine adds each newly formed increment to the destination compartment. The channel solubility check multiplies solubility in mg/L by water volume in m³ and compares the resulting gram-scale number directly with a mass in kg. The threshold is therefore 1,000 times the equivalent threshold in kg. [Water reaction and exchanges](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_rtpest.f90#L118-L198), [Solubility check and bed reaction](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_rtpest.f90#L201-L236), [Database units](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/pesticide_data_module.f90#L9-L13).
- **Skipped calculations.** The normal SD controller can fill the output row even when its inflow test skips pesticide routing. The channel's metabolite fields can then retain earlier values because their record is reset inside `ch_rtpest`. The other process diagnostics are copied from shared scratch fields and can retain values from a previous channel or day when their assignments are skipped. [Routing gate](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L191-L250), [Channel record reset](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/ch_rtpest.f90#L44-L47), [Scratch copies](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/sd_channel_control3.f90#L441-L467).

## Daily values for reservoirs

- **Inflow, split, and storage.** The reservoir report reads the incoming routed pesticide mass and starts with the stored water and bed masses. The routine removes dissolved export first. It then calculates sorbed export from the remaining water pesticide mass. Both calculations divide by the remaining reservoir water volume after the release and other water-budget deductions. Their sum is the load passed to routing when the routine runs. The water and bed fields are the remaining masses. [Release and water budget](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_control.f90#L173-L201), [Pesticide call and outgoing record](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_control.f90#L214-L241), [Inlet and starting storage](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_pest.f90#L40-L49), [Sequential exports and storage](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_pest.f90#L172-L203).
- **Process masses and metabolites.** The reservoir calculates volatilization, settling, resuspension, diffusion, reactions, and burial in the same record as export and storage. Diffusion is positive from the bed to the water. The sediment reaction branch books daughter production to `metab_h2o_kg` and uses the parent water-reaction field and soil formation fraction. The `metab_benth_kg` slot stays at zero. Both branches add the daughter's cumulative diagnostic to storage on each update, so a later update can add mass already counted earlier. The source does not reset this daily reservoir diagnostic between calls. [Water reaction and daughter update](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_pest.f90#L71-L86), [Reservoir transfers](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_pest.f90#L89-L143), [Bed reaction, daughters, and burial](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_pest.f90#L145-L170), [Daily record accumulation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_pesticide_output.f90#L24-L37).
- **Low remaining water and bypass.** At 1 m³ or less of remaining water, the operating reservoir skips the pesticide balance. Its daily report can retain values from an earlier day, while the outgoing pesticide payload comes from the controller's zeroed scratch record. Before construction, the reservoir instead passes the incoming payload through without computing these daily diagnostics. The basin sum can include any retained individual records. [Low-volume gate](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_pest.f90#L39-L49), [Outgoing initialization](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_control.f90#L39-L46), [Outgoing payload](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_control.f90#L214-L241), [Preconstruction bypass](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/res_control.f90#L252-L259), [Basin sum](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/basin_res_pest_output.f90#L25-L31).
