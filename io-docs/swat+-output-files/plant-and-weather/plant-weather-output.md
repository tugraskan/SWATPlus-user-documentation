# Plant and Weather Output

Plant and weather output can be printed at basin, LSU, HRU, and HRU-lte level by entering "y" in the _basin\_pw_, _lsunit\_pw_, _hru\_pw_, and _hru-lte\_pw_ lines in [**print.prt**](../../introduction-1/simulation-settings/print.prt), respectively. Plant and weather  output can be printed at daily, monthly, yearly, and average annual time steps. The names of the plant and weather  output files are as follows:&#x20;

* basin\_pw\_day.txt
* basin\_pw\_mon.txt
* basin\_pw\_yr.txt
* basin\_pw\_aa.txt
* lsunit\_pw\_day.txt
* lsunit\_pw\_mon.txt
* lsunit\_pw\_yr.txt
* lsunit\_pw\_aa.txt
* hru\_pw\_day.txt
* hru\_pw\_mon.txt
* hru\_pw\_yr.txt
* hru\_pw\_aa.txt
* hru-lte\_pw\_day.txt
* hru-lte\_pw\_mon.txt
* hru-lte\_pw\_yr.txt
* hru-lte\_pw\_aa.txt

Set `csvout = y` to open the matching `.csv` files. Daily data rows also require the daily print window and interval to be active. All files use 34 headings and a 32-entry units record. Full HRU rows write 34 values, including the trailing `plant_cov` and `mgt_ops` labels. Basin, LSU, and HRU-LTE rows write 32 values and stop at `c_gro`.

In SWAT+ main `6f515368`, unit 2174 receives the headings and units for `lsunit_pw_day.csv`. The daily data writer sends its rows to unit 2175, leaving the daily CSV without data rows. When monthly CSV output is enabled, `lsunit_pw_mon.csv` can contain both daily and monthly rows. Use `lsunit_pw_day.txt` for daily LSU rows with this revision. [File openers](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/output_landscape_init.f90#L946-L973), [daily CSV write](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/lsu_output.f90#L94-L99), [monthly CSV write](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/lsu_output.f90#L139-L146).

Monthly and yearly rows sum daily yield, stress contributions, and `percn`. The average-annual rows divide those sums by `time%yrs_prt`. For fields that use a day divisor, including `bm_grow` and `c_gro`, the writers use the full calendar month length, the year-end day number (`time%day_end_yr`), or the count of printed days (`time%days_prt`), respectively. The monthly and yearly divisors can therefore include days before a mid-period simulation start. `phubas0` is a dimensionless accumulated heat-unit fraction, printed with the unit label `degc`. Solar radiation is in MJ/m2 (megajoules), printed as `mj/m^2`. [Period divisors in the HRU writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_output.f90#L101-L113), [period operators](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/output_landscape_module.f90#L1323-L1383).

LSU and basin daily rows form weighted sums of their contributing HRU and HRU-LTE records. For longer periods, full HRU and LSU rows report end-of-period plant nitrogen stock, including at simulation end for the average-annual row. Basin rows use the day divisor for nitrogen. The shared addition operator drops `pplnt` (plant phosphorus stock), leaving zero in every LSU and basin period. Full HRUs report current phosphorus stock in all four periods. [Full-HRU writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_output.f90#L145-L153), [LSU writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/lsu_output.f90#L33-L59), [basin writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/basin_output.f90#L28-L59), [addition operator](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/output_landscape_module.f90#L1063-L1091).

HRU-LTE explicitly writes zero for `residue`, `sol_tmp`, `strsn`, `strsp`, `strss`, `nplt`, `percn`, and `pplnt`. Its `bm_max`, `bm_grow`, and `c_gro` stay at their zero defaults. `lai_max` stays at its zero default at all four levels. Full HRUs track monthly and yearly biomass peaks, but the average-annual `bm_max` can miss a new peak on the final day. LSU and basin period `bm_max` values are daily averages of weighted daily biomass. [HRU-LTE daily assignments](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_lte_control.f90#L403-L424), [record defaults](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/output_landscape_module.f90#L195-L221), [full-HRU maximum tracking](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_output.f90#L43-L51), [average-annual write](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_output.f90#L286-L296).

The yearly and average-annual accumulated plant-and-weather values omit a final partial month. The day divisors still include those days. This affects yield, stress, leaching, growth, and weather fields that use the accumulators. Full-HRU and landscape-unit nutrient stock fields follow the end-of-period rules above. [Month transfer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_output.f90#L100-L113), [year and day counts](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/time_control.f90#L138-L148), [end-of-simulation stocks](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_output.f90#L286-L296).

## Landscape-unit and basin contributors

[ls_unit.def](../../introduction-1/landscape-units/ls_unit.def/README.md) supplies the list of element IDs for each landscape unit. [ls_unit.ele](../../introduction-1/landscape-units/ls_unit.ele/README.md) stores each element's type, object number, and two area fractions by element ID. The reader keeps element IDs and object numbers as separate values. [Definition reader](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/lsu_read_elements.f90#L63-L87), [Element reader](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/lsu_read_elements.f90#L97-L130)

| Report | How the writer chooses and weights contributors |
| --- | --- |
| Landscape unit (`lsunit_pw_*`) | For each ID in the landscape-unit list, reads the type and `ru_frac` from that element and uses the same ID to index the daily HRU or HRU-LTE record. The writer skips `ru_frac` values at or below `1.e-9`. |
| Basin (`basin_pw_*`) | Starts at the first element slot in both loops. The loop lengths are the counts of full HRUs and HRU-LTEs. Each loop takes the object number from the visited slot, then uses that number to look up the type, `bsn_frac`, and daily HRU record. It requires the selected type to match the loop and the fraction to exceed `1.e-12`. |

For example, if element 3 names HRU 7, the landscape-unit writer uses HRU record 3 and the fraction from element 3. If the basin loop visits element 3, it goes to record 7 and takes the type and fraction from element 7. Where element IDs differ from object numbers, this can select the wrong record or weight, omit a contributor, or count the same record more than once. The writers use the fractions as supplied and do not divide by their sum. This selection also feeds the monthly, yearly, and average-annual records. The same loops feed Water Balance, Nutrient Balance, and Losses. [Landscape-unit contributors](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/lsu_output.f90#L32-L65), [Basin contributors](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/basin_output.f90#L28-L65)

Landscape-unit rows use the definition's position for `unit`, the name read from `ls_unit.def`, and the GIS ID of the routing-unit object at the same position. The GIS lookup is inside the contributor loop, so a definition with no elements can retain the prior lookup value. Basin rows use `1` for both identifiers. [Definition reader](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/lsu_read_elements.f90#L63-L87), [Landscape-unit lookup and write](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/lsu_output.f90#L32-L97), [Basin write](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/basin_output.f90#L90-L95)

## Daily plant values

In a full HRU, `lai` is the sum of leaf area indices across all plants in the community, calculated before that day's plant growth. The biomass, nitrogen, and phosphorus columns read the community mass record, which is also assembled before growth. The controller copies biomass to the output record before setting a negative community biomass to zero. Residue comes from the surface residue mass record. Soil temperature comes directly from layer 2, and `percn` comes from nitrate leached out of the last soil layer. [Community sums](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/pl_community.f90#L61-L89), [Order of community and growth calls](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_control.f90#L423-L446), [Daily output assignments](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_control.f90#L840-L869), [Bottom-layer nitrate](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/nut_nlch.f90#L139-L148)

The full-HRU `yield` column copies one shared harvest result and then clears it. Harvest routines for grain, biomass, and tubers replace that result, so the last such assignment can overwrite an earlier one on the same day. The HRU-LTE `yield` column stays at zero. Its controller clears a local yield variable every day, while the growing-season end action writes its calculated harvest to a separate HRU-LTE state field. The HRU-LTE `lai` and `bioms` columns read the current growth state. A season-end action can reset both before they are reported. [Harvest result and reset](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_control.f90#L841-L849), [Grain harvest](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/mgt_harvgrain.f90#L46-L54), [Biomass harvest](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/mgt_harvbiomass.f90#L42-L53), [Tuber harvest](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/mgt_harvtuber.f90#L44-L63), [HRU-LTE harvest action](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/actions.f90#L802-L829), [HRU-LTE output assignments](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_lte_control.f90#L403-L424)

Full-HRU stress uses `1 - stress factor` for growing, non-dormant plants whose heat-unit fraction is at most one. It averages five stresses over eligible plants and sums salt stress across them. When none qualify, all six values are zero. HRU-LTE also converts its factors with `1 - factor`. Its water and temperature factors start at zero and are calculated only in the growing branch, so a day that skips that branch reports water and temperature stress of 1 and aeration stress of 0. [Full-HRU stress calculation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_control.f90#L456-L478), [HRU-LTE daily defaults](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_lte_control.f90#L103-L108), [HRU-LTE growth and stress](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_lte_control.f90#L215-L291), [HRU-LTE stress assignments](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_lte_control.f90#L409-L414)

## Daily weather and heat units

Full-HRU weather columns come from the local weather record, with the HRU's elevation lapse adjustment applied to temperatures when enabled. The full-HRU `tmpav` uses the station mean computed before the monthly climate temperature adjustment, plus the lapse adjustment when enabled. HRU-LTE recomputes the mean from the station's adjusted maximum and minimum and uses no HRU-specific lapse adjustment. Both paths take solar radiation, wind speed, and relative humidity from station weather. [Station mean temperature](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/climate_control.f90#L61-L88), [Monthly climate adjustments](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/climate_control.f90#L258-L275), [Full-HRU lapse adjustment](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_control.f90#L117-L132), [HRU-LTE temperature calculation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_lte_control.f90#L93-L105)

The full-HRU `phubas0` adds positive local mean temperature divided by the weather generator's annual potential heat units when those units exceed 0.01. The regular reset falls after reporting on day 181 south of the equator or at year end elsewhere. Some harvest operations also reset it. HRU-LTE instead reports the station's fraction, calculated before monthly climate adjustments. The station resets the fraction before output on the day marked as year end, including a final partial year. [Full-HRU addition](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_control.f90#L229-L232), [Southern reset](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/time_control.f90#L249-L261), [Northern reset](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/time_control.f90#L356-L364), [Harvest reset](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/actions.f90#L618-L627), [Station calculation and reset](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/climate_control.f90#L246-L256), [Year-end flag](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/time_control.f90#L177-L188)
