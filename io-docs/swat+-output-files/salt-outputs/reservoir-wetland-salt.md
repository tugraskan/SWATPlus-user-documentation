# Reservoir and wetland salt

The `reservoir_salt_*` and `wetland_salt_*` files share one layout across daily, monthly, yearly, and average-annual TXT and CSV output.

## How to produce these files

Configure salts and select the desired periods on `res_salt` in [print.prt](../../introduction-1/simulation-settings/print.prt/README.md). For wetland files, also select the same periods on `wetland_salt`. The wetland file opener uses `wetland_salt` and the wetland row writer uses `res_salt`. Both file openers require a reservoir object. Wetland rows come from full HRUs with a wetland assignment. Set `csvout` to `y` for CSV copies. Both daily writers use their daily switch after the skipped years, without a separate day window or interval. [Reservoir file opener](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_salt.f90#L580), [Wetland file opener](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_salt.f90#L852), [Output caller](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/command.f90#L440), [Wetland writer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wet_salt_output.f90#L41)

## Column order

The first six headings are `jday`, `mon`, `day`, `yr`, `unit`, and `gis_id`. The header has 71 columns. The data writers emit one value per configured salt ion in each group and a single water volume, giving 71 row values with eight ions. [Header declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_salt_module.f90#L42), [Reservoir data write](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_salt_output.f90#L41), [Wetland data write](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wet_salt_output.f90#L42)

## Daily reservoir values

The salt calculation runs after the reservoir water balance when stored water exceeds 1 m³. It reads the routed salt inflow and uses the previously stored salt concentration for both release and seepage. It caps the release load against stored plus incoming salt, then caps the seepage load against the remaining mass. The ending concentration uses the resulting salt mass and current reservoir volume. [Water balance and salt call](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_control.f90#L167-L226), [Reservoir salt balance](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_salt.f90#L41-L83)

The reservoir can print a nonzero release load and subtract it from storage while routing zero salt downstream. Immediately after the salt routine assigns the outgoing load, the reservoir controller replaces it with the zero salt array from its outgoing constituent record. Before the reservoir is constructed, the controller passes the incoming load through without running this salt calculation. [Outgoing record initialization](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_control.f90#L39-L42), [Outgoing salt load](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_salt.f90#L78-L86), [Controller overwrite](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_control.f90#L222-L241), [Preconstruction routing](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_control.f90#L260-L265)

When the routine runs with 1 m³ or less, it clears the printed inflow, release, seepage, mass, and concentration. The printed volume can retain a previous value, and the routine leaves stored salt as it was. With GWFlow salt exchange enabled, GWFlow changes stored salt directly before the reservoir calculation. The reported seepage load comes from a separate calculation using signed net water seepage and the previously stored concentration. [Low-volume branch](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_salt.f90#L30-L83), [GWFlow salt exchange](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/gwflow_reservoir.f90#L104-L215)

Reservoir withdrawals through water allocation can reduce water storage without removing stored salt. [Reservoir withdrawal](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wallo_withdraw.f90#L100-L109)

## Daily wetland values

The wetland uses the water volume remaining after its water balance and the existing salt mass to calculate the concentration used for release and seepage. Its inflow column counts salt from routed surface runon. It caps losses first for release and then for seepage. If the wetland is dry at the salt calculation, it uses zero concentration for both losses and can still retain incoming salt in storage. [Water balance and salt call](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wetland_control.f90#L183-L232), [Wetland salt balance](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wet_salt.f90#L39-L87)

The printed release load is measured before the HRU's surface runoff lag. The wetland adds the seepage load to the top soil layer. Wetland fertilizer applications add salt to storage at each application, while each application replaces the daily fertilizer value. [Release and seepage transfers](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wet_salt.f90#L89-L95), [HRU runoff lag](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/stor_surfstor.f90#L158-L231), [Wetland fertilizer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/salt_fert_wet.f90#L31-L49)

## Reporting periods

Both writers omit a final partial month from yearly and average-annual flux, stored-mass, concentration, and volume accumulations. Yearly state values still use the final day-of-year divisor. [Reservoir month transfer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_salt_output.f90#L66-L84), [wetland month transfer](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wet_salt_output.f90#L67-L85).

- **Daily.** The wetland TXT preamble calls stored mass, concentration, and volume beginning-of-day values. The salt calculation writes the mass and concentration after accounting for inflow, outflow, and seepage, and takes volume from the current wetland water storage. The reservoir calculation also records the mass and concentration after its salt balance. [Wetland TXT preamble](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_salt.f90#L869), [Wetland state calculation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wet_salt.f90#L69), [Reservoir state calculation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_salt.f90#L69)
- **Monthly and yearly.** The writers sum the mass transfers in kg and divide the daily sums of stored mass, concentration, and water volume by the calendar month length or `time%day_end_yr`, respectively. [Reservoir aggregation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_salt_output.f90#L66), [Wetland aggregation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wet_salt_output.f90#L67)
- **Average annual.** The writers divide the daily sums by the configured number of simulation years (`time%nbyr`). Mass transfers and the sum of daily stored mass are labeled kg/yr in the TXT preamble. The stored mass, concentration, and volume values represent daily sums per configured year. [Reservoir annual scaling](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/res_salt_output.f90#L182), [Wetland annual scaling](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/wet_salt_output.f90#L183), [Reservoir TXT preamble](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_salt.f90#L679), [Wetland TXT preamble](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/header_salt.f90#L951)
