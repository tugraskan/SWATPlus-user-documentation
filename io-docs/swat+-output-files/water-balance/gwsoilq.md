# gwsoilq

**Code reference:** `bwb_d%gwsoil` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/output_landscape_module.f90#L38)).

Groundwater transferred into full-HRU soil layers by the GWFlow soil exchange routine. The daily value sums the depth increments added to the layers. HRU-LTE reports zero. [Calculation and reporting details](README.md#evapotranspiration-and-soil-water)

**Unit:** mm.
