# pet

**Code reference:** `bwb_d%pet` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/output_landscape_module.f90#L26)).

Full HRUs use the configured basin calculation or supplied series, scaled by the HRU PET coefficient. HRU-LTE applies its own ET coefficient to its Hargreaves or Priestley–Taylor calculation. [Calculation and reporting details](README.md#snow-irrigation-and-drainage)
