# snofall

**Code reference:** `bwb_d%snofall` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/output_landscape_module.f90#L7)).

In a full HRU, this is effective precipitation after canopy interception when mean temperature is at or below the configured snowfall threshold. In HRU-LTE, this is station precipitation when mean temperature is below 0 °C. [Calculation and reporting details](README.md#snow-irrigation-and-drainage)

**Unit:** mm.
