# sw_300

**Code reference:** `bwb_d%sw_300` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/output_landscape_module.f90#L22)).

The full-HRU controller sums layer water depths down to 300 mm and prorates the layer that crosses that boundary. HRU-LTE instead writes `hlt(isd)%sw / 300.`, a ratio based on the single soil-water store. Basin and LSU records can combine these two different quantities. [full-HRU depth](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_control.f90#L480-L495), [HRU-LTE ratio](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_lte_control.f90#L381-L385).

**Unit:** mm for full HRUs. The HRU-LTE ratio is dimensionless and retains the printed mm label.

The same printed heading can appear in daily, monthly, yearly, and average-annual variants of this family. The reporting interval changes the row period; this page does not infer a total-versus-average rule beyond what the family output and source establish.

Storage timing is writer-dependent. The [HRU routine](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/hru_output.f90#L40) adds the daily water-balance record to the monthly accumulator before updating daily soil-water and snowpack means. It advances initial daily stores inside the day-print gate. The [family warning](README.md) explains why the nominal heading alone does not guarantee boundary timing or equality with a mean of printed daily rows. These source-order concerns have not been runtime-tested.
