# wet_oflo

**Code reference:** `bwb_d%wet_out` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/aa4179fea9c9fbb9b00efc38e3d4def3b6a3f715/src/output_landscape_module.f90#L46)).

The full-HRU wetland release diagnostic is the water volume in the release record converted to a depth. A shallow wetland weir can add excess soil water to that record. HRU-LTE reports zero. [Calculation and reporting details](README.md#run-on-and-water-yield)
